## Summary

Defines sprite sheet coordinate lookups for two icon systems: VESSEL_SPRITE_ICON_MAPPING (vessel, highlighted, circle marker with masking enabled) and GAPS_SPRITE_ICON_MAPPING (off/on toggle states with custom anchors, masking disabled). Enables efficient icon rendering by pinpointing pre-composed graphics within sprite sheets rather than loading individual images.
